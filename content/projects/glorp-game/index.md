A game my friends and I are working on, with the plan of releasing it on steam as our own self-published product.

The idea is a silly physics-based co-op game where you explore a procedurally generated, disaster-stricken spaceship with your friends and have to solve a few logic and physics puzzles along the way, with the goal of saving any survivors.

I've been learning a ton about multiplayer in this project, specifically the Unreal Replication system and such, how to keep visuals on clients responsive while dealing with the slight delay of the actual logic happening.
